Methodology, position estimates and limitations

ADL scope: Historical MFL weekly rosters for league 60206, seasons 2022–2025, weeks 1–17. The 2021 season is excluded because reconstructed Out labels substantially undercount MFL Out designations. Only ROSTER players count; taxi squad and ADL injured reserve are excluded. Players held on Active Roster with Suspended (S), Holdout (H), I, or any IR designation (including IR-R, IR-PUP and IR-NFI) are counted as Mispl. instead of ACT or INA, even on a bye. ACT + INA + Mispl. equals the ADL Active Roster total. Remaining Active Roster players with O/Out or a Bye are INA; everyone else is ACT. Historical MFL injury exports supply Out. All 68 displayed season-weeks use direct MFL injury records. Where those exports are empty or unavailable, archived nflreadr injury reports reconstruct Out; this is an estimate of the historical ADL designation. The local player audit identifies every source; the public coverage download reports designation sources by week. When MFL lists the same player under multiple roster statuses in one franchise-week, count the player once and prefer explicit ROSTER membership; conflicting source statuses remain in the local audit and weekly coverage totals. Byes come from the NFL schedule using same-week or nearest-week NFL team observations, with annual MFL team fallback flagged.

Comparison denominators: ADL averages include every franchise-week, including players on NFL byes. NFL averages include played team-games only. Both sides use weeks 1–17. These are comparable roster-position counts, but ADL INA is the requested Out/Bye definition, not an independently verified NFL gameday inactive list. Each franchise/team and season receives equal weight.

Roster scope: Completed regular-season team-games only. Byes and postseason are excluded. The canceled 2022 BUF–CIN game is excluded, so those teams have one fewer observed game in the comparison window. All original statuses are preserved in the source snapshot; only ACT and INA contribute to the composition benchmark. Status labels are source-reported and are not independently verified against gamebooks.

Averages: Count each player once per team-game, including zero position counts. Average games within each team-season, then give all 32 teams equal weight for the whole-NFL season. The displayed four-year composite equally weights the four season means. Values represent an average NFL team, not a sum across the entire league. Two decimals are presentation rounding.

Identity and position: Link nflreadr players to MFL IDs through GSIS, ESPN, Sportradar, PFR and Rotowire identifiers, plus direct ESPN/Rotowire identifiers in the annual ADL export. Conflicting candidates are not accepted. Use the matched MFL player’s position from that year’s league 60206 player export, never today’s position. Annual exports reflect the database as retrieved, not intra-season weekly MFL position history.

Fallback: For missing or unusable MFL matches, estimate from the most common MFL position among matched player classifications in the same season and NFL position/depth-position group. Count distinct player classifications, rather than weeks, when learning that schema. Save support and agreement share in the audit. If the group has no matched examples, translate FB→RB, K→PK, P→PN, NT→DT, ILB/MLB/OLB→LB and FS/SS→S; use the other explicit depth positions directly. Unresolved generic DB/DL stays UNMAPPED. NFL OL and LS remain non-ADL regardless of any MFL listing.

Remaining: ACT + INA − OL − LS. Any UNMAPPED players remain part of this total; their position is uncertain. This is an observed roster benchmark, not an adopted ADL roster rule or a reconstructed permanent 53-player roster.

Frozen snapshot: Refresh is explicit; ordinary dashboard builds reuse the saved data.

Sources: nflreadr weekly rosters, fantasy player ID crosswalk, NFL roster rules.
