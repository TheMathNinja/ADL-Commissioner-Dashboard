Coverage and methodology

Historical seasons cover weeks 1–17. The current season stops at the latest week present in the frozen NFL snap-count source. The same-weeks control restricts every season to those week numbers so early-season choices can be compared consistently. All regular-season team-games present in the source count; playoff games and NFL bye weeks do not. ADL submissions on franchise byes are included when a complete lineup is returned by MFL.

NFL players are linked through stable PFR/MFL IDs and assigned their season-specific league 60206 position. Missing matches use the most common ADL position among distinct matched players with that NFL position in that year; explicit NFL translations are the final fallback. Position-match coverage is available below. NFL side eligibility follows the ADL position: for example, a player listed as an ADL DT but used as an NFL fullback is not eligible for the offensive group. Annual player databases reflect positions when retrieved, not changes within the season.

The top-N cutoff follows the existing PositionLimits.R approach: players above the cutoff count fully and tied players split the remaining slots. Formation frequencies enumerate the possible integer position combinations among tied players, weighted by the number of player combinations. Their average position counts exactly reproduce the split-slot averages.

ADL uses MFL weeklyResults players marked “starter,” never the “shouldStart” optimal-lineup flag. Kicker, punter and non-individual positions are excluded. A complete offensive lineup has 7 relevant starters; a complete defensive lineup has 12. Missing or incomplete sides are excluded from averages and formation frequencies and reported in the coverage download. Both sources are averaged within each team, then equally across teams.

Source: nflreadr / PFR snap counts, public MFL weeklyResults and annual player exports.
